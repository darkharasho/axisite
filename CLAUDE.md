## Project Context

axiwiki is the front-page landing site for the axi suite of apps. It acts as a central hub linking out to every axi app and web property — including the ACRDPS wiki and the addon checker — with consistent branding from the shared axi-design style system. It lives alongside the other axi projects in Docs/Github.

## Goals

- Serve as the single front door / landing page for the axi suite
- Provide a browsable hub of all axi apps and web pages (ACRDPS wiki, addon checker, and future additions)
- Use the existing axi-design style system so the hub matches the rest of the suite
- Make adding a new app or page to the hub trivial (data-driven listing)
