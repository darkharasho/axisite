AxiStream is an opinionated Windows and Linux desktop app that gets a Guild Wars 2 player live on YouTube in about three clicks. It drives an AxiStream-owned OBS Studio runtime to capture a display, hardware-encode with GW2-tuned bitrate presets, and stream to YouTube over a signed-in OAuth connection. User-positioned privacy masks keep chat and DMs off the stream, audio is configured in-app with device pickers, and stream-health chips warn you when the encoder is struggling.
