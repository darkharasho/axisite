AxiPulse is a lightweight desktop companion that watches your arcdps log folder, parses each fight locally in-process, and shows you exactly how you performed while the squad is still forming up for the next one. Its Pulse view gives a per-fight snapshot of damage, DPS, down contribution, strips, cleanses, and healing, ranked against the squad, and a Timeline view replays the fight second by second. No browser tabs, no uploads, no waiting — drop it on a second monitor and let it work.
