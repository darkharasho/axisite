AxiForge is an Electron app for creating Guild Wars 2 builds, publishing a static build site, and syncing it to GitHub Pages. Its native build editor covers profession, three specialization lines with trait picks, heal/utility/elite skills, and optional equipment notes and tags, driven by a data-driven editor catalog pulled from the GW2 API. Team Sync lets teammates share folders and sync edits in real time.
