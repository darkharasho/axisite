arcdps-team-breakdown is a Rust arcdps plugin for Guild Wars 2 WvW that renders two ImGui overlay windows: a stacked-bar summary of player counts across Squad, Allies, Enemy A, and Enemy B, and a per-spec class breakdown of cross-team damage sorted highest first. A Fight/Session toggle controls the damage scope, and a reset hotkey or map change wipes the accumulated data.
