AxiRoster is a dedicated Guild Wars 2 WvW guild roster management desktop app for guild leadership. It auto-pulls GW2 guild rosters and Discord members via the AxiTools bot, reconciles in-game accounts, Discord members, and manual links into one row per person, and overlays AxiBridge combat reports — main class, class spread, raid attendance, and last seen. A guild leader can share the roster live across connected officers, with role-based read/write access backed by Supabase sync.
