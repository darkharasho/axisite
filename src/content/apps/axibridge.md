AxiBridge watches your arcdps log folder and automatically uploads every fight as it ends, then summarizes WvW fight breakdowns — squad vs. enemy sizes, damage, downs/deaths, cleanses, strips, stability, and healing. It posts clean Discord-ready embeds or shareable report images, identifies top performers by role and stat category, and can publish a persistent web report over GitHub Pages so your squad can review it anytime.
