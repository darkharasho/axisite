arcdps-player-outline is an arcdps plugin for Guild Wars 2 that draws a persistent, always-on-top marker on your own character so you can instantly find yourself in a crowded zerg. It reads your position from MumbleLink and projects it to screen space, tracking smoothly at any camera angle with six marker styles — a projected ground ring, adaptive silhouette glow, overhead chevron, light beam, and combinations of ring, pip, and chevron. It marks only you: it does not outline other players or NPCs, and does not show distance to the commander tag.
