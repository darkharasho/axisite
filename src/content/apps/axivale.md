AxiVale is a virtual officer for your Guild Wars 2 guild and Discord — a Claude agent with real tools, styled as a dark-newsprint broadsheet. You file orders in plain English, like "who joined this week?" or "add the MetaBattle feed to #news," and it does the work: pulling guild intelligence, join history, and activity through the official GW2 API, and handling builds, comps, roster, Discord management, audits, and announcements.
