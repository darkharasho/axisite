AxiTools is a multi-guild Discord bot that helps Guild Wars 2 communities organise and share build information while respecting each server's privacy requirements, built as modular cogs so specific slash-command behaviours are easy to extend or maintain. Per-guild configuration lets administrators choose which roles can interact with the bot and where build posts land, `/builds` manages adding, editing, and deleting builds with profession, chat codes, and audit metadata, and `/rss` and dev-tracker subscriptions post new GW2 update notes automatically. `/comp manage` also schedules recurring composition posts with signup tracking synced to reactions.
