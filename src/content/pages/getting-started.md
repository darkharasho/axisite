title: Getting started
description: Install AxiOM, then add the apps you want.

---

Start with [AxiOM](/apps/axiom/). It's the launcher: it shows every app in the suite, installed or not, and handles installing and updating from one click, so you don't need to track down individual releases by hand. Everything else in axi is optional and installed through it.

After that, pick apps by what you actually do:

- **Play WvW and want to know how a fight went?** [axilog](/apps/axilog/) parses your arcdps logs, [arcdps_axipulse](/apps/arcdps-axipulse/) and [AxiPulse](/apps/axipulse/) put live and post-fight stats in front of you without leaving the game, and [AxiBridge](/apps/axibridge/) posts fight reports to your squad's Discord automatically.
- **Building or sharing character builds?** [AxiForge](/apps/axiforge/) is the build editor and publishes a static build site; [AxiCode](/apps/axicode/) is the encoder underneath it if you need build strings elsewhere.
- **Running a Discord community or guild?** [AxiTools](/apps/axitools/) organizes builds and update posts per server, and [AxiRoster](/apps/axiroster/) reconciles in-game and Discord membership for guild leadership.
- **Going live?** [AxiStream](/apps/axistream/) gets you streaming to YouTube in a few clicks.

You don't need all of them — most people land on two or three. The [suite](/) page lists everything with its current status, so it's worth a look before you commit to an install.
